# Changelog

### Breaking Changes 🚨

- `5f02220` The standing charge is billed by the day, as suppliers do: every day costs twelve months over 365 days, whatever its month. A bill from the 12th to the 11th now matches the supplier's to the cent; a single month no longer shows exactly the entered monthly amount (February less, months with 31 days more), while a year stays the same. **All statistics are computed again on the first start after the update.** This takes a while; until it is done, the cards show the progress and the figures of the past change as it goes. ([#65](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/65))

### Other Changes ☀️

- `5f02220` The electricity bill's table lists each line once: where price levels occur, they take the place of grid import, so the total is the only sum. Its values are exact, kWh with a tenth and money with its cents, to check them against the bill; the total's average price includes the standing charge. ([#65](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/65))
