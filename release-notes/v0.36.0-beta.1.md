# Changelog

### Breaking Changes 🚨

- `45cccc5` A home battery keeps where its energy came from: what it charged from the grid is grid power when it is used, at what it cost, with the battery's losses; what it charged from solar power is solar power. With grid charging, the electricity mix shows less solar power and the costs are higher than before. **All statistics are computed again on the first start after the update.** This takes a while; until it is done, the cards show the progress and the figures of the past change as it goes. ([#75](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/75))

### New Features 💫

- `76e29f3` German solar plants commissioned from 2009 to March 2012 can enter their self-consumption bonus (Eigenverbrauchsvergütung, EEG 2009) and its last day. Solar power used on site then costs the feed-in compensation less the bonus, and the bonus is part of the solar saving, with its own row on the balance card. ([#76](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/76))
- `d6ef9b3` The electric car takes a reimbursement per kWh, for a company car whose charging at home the employer pays. It is income of the car and part of its saving; a reimbursed car compared with nothing now has a saving too. ([#77](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/77))

### Bug Fixes 🐞

- `45cccc5` Batteries measured per direction, as Home Assistant 2026.12 allows, no longer stop the integration. ([#75](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/75))
