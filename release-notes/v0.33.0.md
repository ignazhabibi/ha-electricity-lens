# Changelog

### Breaking Changes 🚨

- `8178603` Price levels follow the grid price on their own: cheap is at least 10 % below the normal price, the median of the last 7 days, expensive at least 10 % above. The options "Cheap up to" and "Expensive from" are gone, and the household is computed again once. ([#42](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/42))

### New Features 💫

- `8178603` The costs view shows "Grid cheap" with the share of cheap hours and the tariff saving, which now counts cheap hours against expensive ones. ([#42](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/42))
