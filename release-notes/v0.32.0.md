# Changelog

### Breaking Changes 🚨

- `077fe16` New installations name some entities differently: the English solar category is "Solar" instead of "PV plant", so its sensors are `sensor.solar_*`, and the forecast sensors are "Solar left today" and "Solar tomorrow" ("PV heute noch", "PV morgen"). Existing installations keep their entity IDs; only the names they show change. ([#36](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/36))

### New Features 💫

- `077fe16` An info icon next to the title explains what the numbers count, as on Home Assistant's self-sufficiency gauge: on the power flow, economics, savings, COP by device, costs by source, the electricity bill, and the price, heat price, payback, and return key figures. ([#36](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/36))

### Other Changes ☀️

- `077fe16` One wording on cards, in the card editors, the setup, entities, and statistics: German says PV, English solar; the heat pump's electricity and electricity costs, the car's charged energy and charging costs; COP instead of JAZ; devices instead of consumers; Total under every table. ([#36](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/36))
- `077fe16` Titles follow one rule and fit their place: for example Power flow, Energy flow, Costs by source, COP by device. The savings table has short rows, and the heat pump's and car's reckoning names the comparison, such as Gas heating costs. ([#36](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/36))
- `077fe16` The tables keep their color swatches in half columns on a desktop and hide them only on phones. ([#36](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/36))

### Bug Fixes 🐞

- `077fe16` The key figure editor no longer calls the car's charged energy "consumption", which there means kWh per 100 km. ([#36](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/36))
