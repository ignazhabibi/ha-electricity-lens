# Changelog

### New Features 💫

- `fc3f678` A heat pump in a new build can be set up without a former heating: it shows its heat, seasonal COP, heat price, and the Heating view, without a saving or a place in the balance. ([#30](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/30))
- `fc3f678` An electric car can be compared with nothing, for example a second car: it shows its charging costs, mix, and the Charging view, and asks only for an own tariff. ([#30](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/30))
- `fc3f678` The economics card says how much of all investments is paid back and about how long it takes until everything is in the plus; the balance sensor has both as attributes. ([#30](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/30))
- `fc3f678` The solar forecast gauges reach the best solar day of the last twelve months instead of a fixed 70 kWh, so they fit a balcony plant as well as a large roof; they show colors once the data covers a year. ([#30](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/30))

### Other Changes ☀️

- `fc3f678` The economics card's column has the balance sensor's name, a heat pump's or car's saving reckoned up shows the part the balance counts, and the card picker names the cards in German too. ([#30](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/30))
- `fc3f678` The README lists every card and has its options table repaired. ([#30](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/30))

### Bug Fixes 🐞

- `fc3f678` The end of the setup points to the Electricity Lens dashboard instead of a card that does not exist. ([#30](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/30))
- `fc3f678` The dashboard leaves out cards that would show only one share: costs by device without individual devices, rings by source without solar power or price levels. ([#30](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/30))
