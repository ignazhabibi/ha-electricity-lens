# Changelog

### New Features 💫

- `9caa214` A heat pump can now replace LPG, pellets, district heating, or an electric heating such as storage heaters, besides gas and heating oil. Each asks for its price in its own unit. ([#32](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/32))
- `9caa214` The battery has its own part of the PV plant's saving: what it adds to the same plant without it, computed hour by hour. It has a purchase cost and a payback of its own, a row in the economics card, and a key figure; the PV plant's saving and the balance stay the same. ([#32](https://github.com/ignazhabibi/ha-electricity-lens/pull/32))
